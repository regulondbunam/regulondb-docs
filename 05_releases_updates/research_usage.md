# Research Usage

This page summarizes how RegulonDB is used in scientific research, based on citations of the RegulonDB database publications and on documented reuse.

- **Total citations:** 2,901.

## Methodological Reuse

RegulonDB is reused in:

- Gene regulatory network inference and modeling workflows.
- Validation and benchmarking of high-throughput methods for transcription factor binding and transcription start sites; the [gold standard datasets](../02_data_access/gold_standard_guide_en.md) allow users to exclude specific evidence types to avoid circular validation.
- Comparative analyses and cross-resource integration.

Representative examples are described in [Impact Stories](impact_stories.md).

## Reporting Method

- Citation counts are updated at least once a year from Scopus using the same DOI-based query.
- The profile of citing documents (types, journals, countries, research areas) comes from the Scopus export of 31 March 2025.
- Reuse examples are taken from peer-reviewed publications.
